## Mitigation status legend

- **OPEN:** Risk is live, mitigation in place but not yet tested/passed.
- **MITIGATED:** Mitigation executed successfully in this phase. Risk reduced but not eliminated.
- **REALIZED:** Risk occurred and was handled. Document what happened and how it was resolved.
- **CLOSED:** Risk is no longer possible (e.g., pre-registration is public, cannot be changed).
- **ACCEPTED:** Risk is acceptable as-is. Mitigation is too expensive or impossible; we are proceeding anyway with this known limit.
